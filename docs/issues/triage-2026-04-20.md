## Context

Three reference documents shape this triage:

1. **`docs/users/priorities.md`** — Tier 1–4 story stack: foundation (listing, events, browse, trust), core loops (borrow, loan tracking, volunteer self-service), revenue engine (Carmen's quiet giving, Ian's fundraiser), retention (Henry's loop closure, reciprocity).
2. **`docs/users/village-blueprint.md`** — the platform's psychological model: move users from the Transactional Zone to the Village by **rewarding vulnerability**, not just giving; build a **Resilience/Weaver Score** that grows only when users both contribute and ask; apply **scoped transparency** so sensitive asks stay in the inner circle while mundane asks build social proof; treat **friends-of-friends** as a first-class bridge.
3. **`docs/users/growth-use-cases.md`** — the growth model: the inviter is never the seeker. Growth comes from organizers in a giving posture — **Crisis Coordinator** (Betty for Henry/Ian), **Potluck Host** (Emma), **Ride/Trip Sharer**, **Group Gift Organizer**. Every one of these loops requires the invite to ride on a generous act and to work on people who aren't app users yet.

**Priority-shaping implications for this triage:**

- **Seeker protection is now first-class.** Anything that reduces friction on Henry's and Ian's side of the loop (#742 request-response urgency, #603 loan due dates, and the new Undo story in #964) gets elevated — these are the "Village" mechanics that lower the cost of asking.
- **Organizer-driven growth is gated on specific features.** #1195 (login-free web participation) is the enabling surface for Crisis Coordinator, Potluck Host, and Ride Sharer — it is now P1. #1186 (Experience collaborative lists) is the Potluck Host product and moves to P1. #625 (scheduled notifications) is an event-loop prerequisite and moves to P1.
- **Weaver/Resilience scoring is strategic.** #1159 (Measurement v2 migration to Common Wealth / Presence / Stewardship with Give/Ask balance as a resilience lens) implements the village-blueprint's reward-vulnerability principle. Promote to P1.
- **Top-priority personas remain** Alfred (stuff × giving), Betty (time × giving), Carmen (money × giving) — but the framing now explicitly includes **Betty as Crisis Coordinator for Henry/Ian**, which means work that makes Betty's coordination tools sharper is simultaneously seeker-facing.
