# Release 0.26.1

Member reshare fix. Any member of a community an item is shared with can now
reshare it — the growth loop of the phone-first pivot that was broken since
0.26.0.

## Bug Fixes

- **Member Reshare Permission Error**: Fixed "You don't have permission to
  perform this action" shown to non-owner community members when tapping
  **Invite** on gear, events, or requests. The share sheet calls `ShareItem`
  on open to resolve the per-item share link; the RPC previously owner-gated
  its entire body including that link fetch. Link-only calls (no invitees, no
  audience changes) now allow any member with view access, matching the intent
  of the phone-first reshare growth loop. Audience management — inviting
  individuals and sharing to additional communities — stays host-only, and the
  share sheet hides those rows for non-owner members. Each resharer receives
  their own revocable `/go/` link
  ([#2630](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/issues/2630))
