# Release 0.9.0 — Private Beta Launch

**This is the official Ripls Private Beta launch.** After months of closed testing,
iteration, and hardening, Ripls is opening up to our first wave of private beta
users. This release represents a major milestone: a polished, stable, and
feature-complete foundation for community sharing of goods and experiences.

## Highlights

- **Private Beta Ready**: Comprehensive UX polish, bug fixes, and stability
  improvements across every workflow — loans, giveaways, requests, and
  experiences are all production-ready for real communities.
- **Notification Deep Linking**: All notifications now navigate directly to the
  relevant item detail screen, not just a generic chat view
  ([#977](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/issues/977),
  [#962](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/issues/962),
  [#978](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/978),
  [#980](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/980))
- **Nudge Cards**: AI-generated nudge cards in the feed encourage engagement —
  suggesting items to share, experiences to plan, or requests to make
  ([#935](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/935))
- **Feed Freshness**: Smart feed expiration based on view count and time since
  last activity, so the feed always feels current and relevant
- **Expanded Metrics**: Community and personal impact metrics with leaderboards,
  charts, and detailed drill-down views
  ([#975](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/975),
  [#949](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/949),
  [#952](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/952))

## New Features

- **Item Link Previews**: Sharing a Ripls link on social media or messaging apps
  now shows rich OG metadata with item-specific titles, descriptions, and
  thumbnail images
  ([#955](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/955),
  [#953](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/issues/953))
- **Pickup Coordination**: Arrange pickup times directly within loan and
  giveaway conversations with date/time proposals and notifications
- **Time Polls for Experiences**: Organizers can propose multiple times and
  collect votes from attendees, with a "none work for me" option
- **Multi-Model AI**: Switched to Haiku as the primary AI provider with Gemini
  fallback for faster, more cost-effective gear recognition
  ([#918](https://github.com/Type2Fun-Foundation/ripls/pull/918))
