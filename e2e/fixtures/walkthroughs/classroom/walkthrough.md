## 01 · A request to stock a classroom

actor: Maya, the teacher

Room 7 needs donations before the new school year. Ms. Okafor names the
supply list in a sentence — picture books, a whiteboard, storage bins, art
supplies, construction paper — and **Ripls turns each item named into
something a parent can claim**, so five names in one line become five
separate pieces. She shares it to the class parents and copies the link for
the parents' WhatsApp group.

## 02 · Parents offer help

actor: Maya, the teacher

The offers come in: Theo gives a box of picture books, Nadia gives storage
bins, Dana gives an art caddy, and Bea **lends a whiteboard for the year**.
Each is tagged as given or lent on the request, so Ms. Okafor can tell what
she's keeping from what goes back.

## 03 · The last piece

actor: Rob, a parent from the group chat

Rob saw the link in the parents' WhatsApp. He hasn't used Ripls before — he
opens it in his browser, confirms his phone number, and takes the **last open
piece**, a pack of construction paper.

## 04 · Everything delivered

actor: Maya, the teacher

Drop-off day: the parents bring it all by, and Ms. Okafor marks the drive
done. The gifts become the classroom's to keep and the whiteboard is on loan
for the year. **The request keeps the record** — every piece, who brought it,
and an estimate of what it was worth.
