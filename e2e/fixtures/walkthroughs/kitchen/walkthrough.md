## 01 · Point the camera at it

actor: Maya, the offerer

Maya's leaving Friday for a long trip and wants to clear out her pantry. She
snaps the spaghetti, and **the photo becomes the listing** — Ripls writes the
name and description. She switches it from lend to give, saves, and shares it
to her street and the school moms.

## 02 · Clear the whole shelf

actor: Maya, the offerer

The bread and the broccoli go the same way.

## 03 · The first claim lands

actor: Priya, a neighbor

Priya spots the sourdough in her street's circle and taps to say she wants
it. Her note lands in **the item's own conversation**, so Maya and the rest of
the circle can see the loaf is spoken for.

## 04 · Two people want the pasta

actor: Maya, the offerer

Marcus could use it. Then Dana writes: "I literally have a pot of red sauce
simmering right now and zero noodles." **Both messages are on the item**,
where everyone interested can read them, rather than in separate threads Maya
would have to compare.

## 05 · It goes where it's needed most

actor: Maya, the offerer

Maya picks Dana, and the item shows that it's hers — **Marcus can see the
outcome without being told**. Porch pickup at five.

## 06 · Everything found a home

actor: Maya, the offerer

Two days out, all three items are claimed. **Maya marks each one handed off
from her own list**, and Ripls closes them out. Three items, three homes,
nothing thrown away.
