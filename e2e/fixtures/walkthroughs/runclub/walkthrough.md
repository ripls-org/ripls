## 01 · Posting the run

actor: Nadia, the host

Nadia types the run the way she'd text it: what, where, when. Ripls reads the
Wednesday and the 6:30am out of the sentence and fills in the gate she meets
at, and she adds a photo. Saving gives her a link to paste wherever her
runners already are — a group chat, a neighborhood thread, a noticeboard.

## 02 · Joining from the link

actor: Marcus, who was sent the link

Marcus opens the link on his phone. There's no app to install and no account
to make: the run's page loads in his browser, he confirms his number, gives a
first name, and he's on the list. Nadia didn't need his email or have to add
him to anything.

## 03 · Who's coming

actor: Nadia, the host

By Tuesday evening six people have joined, all from the same link being
forwarded around. Nadia can see who's in without asking anyone to reply.

## 04 · Wednesday, 6:30am

actor: Priya, one of the runners

The run happens, and the photos go to the run's own thread rather than a
group chat where they'd scroll away by lunchtime. Priya opens it afterwards
and adds a note; everyone who was there is already in the conversation.

## 05 · Wrapping up

actor: Nadia, the host

Wrapping up is a confirmation rather than a form — everyone who said yes is
ticked already, and Nadia unticks anyone who didn't make it. Ripls records the
time the group spent together, and who came carries forward to next week.

## 06 · Naming the group

actor: Nadia, the host

The seven of them have been a group all week without a name. Nadia gives them
one — Hump Day Milers — and from the group's page she copies an invite link
for the group itself rather than for a single run, so whoever takes it is
there for the following weeks too.

## 07 · Setting up next week

actor: Nadia, the host

Back on the home screen, Ripls offers to schedule the run again. Tapping it
opens next Wednesday already filled in: the same route, the same 6:30am, and
the people who actually showed up. Nadia checks it over and posts it.
