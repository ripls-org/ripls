## The Ripls Foundation

Our mission: strengthen local communities by enabling people to ask for and offer help through open technology.

## Our Principles

### Share what we have

We believe everyone has resources and skills to share with those around them, and that act of sharing helps the giver and the recipient. Communities built around sharing provide for their members, reducing isolation and dependence on buying things and hiring outside help.

### Build human connection

We want to get people offline and into the real world, together. We seek to connect people around shared interests and in order to help their communities thrive.

### Use only free and open technology

We believe that open, transparent and trustworthy technology can promote thriving communities and healthy human relationships. We pledge to build in the open and always and only serve the communities that use our tools.

## The problem

Modern society has shifted from a Reciprocal Economy (favors and trust) to a Transactional Economy (paying strangers). While efficient, this shift has eroded our social capital. Capitalism is making this worse: products instead of experiences, everything must have a money-making angle, and local interdependence is replaced with commercial transactions, which are less satisfying for the giver and receiver.

Technology and the internet, which hold great promise for connection, have recently been making things even worse. For-profit tech companies lock us into walled gardens by holding our social relationships hostage and manipulate our emotions to sell ads and our own data.

It's time to fight fire with fire: we'll reclaim technology for positive social impact, using it to build communities and convert consumerism back to mutual support.

## Our first project: The Ripls tool

To put our principles into action, we've created a first draft of a tool called _Ripls_, which makes it easy to ask for and offer help in ad hoc communities, and to plan group and community events. Ripls is (and will always be) free to use, free of advertising, and free of user data collection and tracking. We plan to publish the _Ripls_ source code so others can host their own instances.

## Our story

We're a group of technologists who have done our time in big tech, seen the damage tech platforms have done to our society, and are ready to help make a change.

We think the root cause of many modern mental and physical health crises, as well as threats to the health of our planet, can be traced by to a lack of strong, local human connection. Consumerism makes this worse: we buy things rather than having experiences, we hire people to bring us food rather than cooking and eating together, we scroll endlessly through faked lives rather than connecting with the real lives of those around us.

To combat these crises, we propose to harness technology for the benefit of our communities. We want tech to give people back to each other, in all our richness, our gifts, and our needs. By making our latent connections visible, accessible, and easy to deepen, technology can be a tool to repair society and improve everyone's life.

In pursuit of these goals, we've formed the non-profit Ripls Foundation (tax-exempt status pending).
