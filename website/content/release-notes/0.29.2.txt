Release 0.29.2


Two bug fixes: a nonsense sharing nudge is gone, and the “say hi” notification link now lands in the community discussion.

Bug Fixes

- Nonsense Sharing Nudge Removed: The Home screen could show a card saying “Share your [item] with the circle — create a sharing event so everyone can borrow it at once.” Four things were wrong: the gear was already shared (that’s why the card existed), the product name was clipped to three words, “sharing event” is not a real concept in Ripls, and tapping the button hit a guaranteed NotFound error every time with no visible feedback. The detector behind it (IdleOfferDetector) also emitted fabricated statistics attributed to real sources. All of this has been removed (#2892)

- “Say Hi” Notification Links to Community Discussion: When a new member joins a group, the welcome notification’s “say hi” link now opens the community discussion thread directly. Previously the link led somewhere that didn’t surface the discussion at all (#2876)
