SMS Opt-In & Consent

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Ripls (operated by Type2Fun Foundation, PBC) sends text messages so people can be invited to events and share things with the communities they're part of. This page explains exactly how you consent to receive those messages, what they look like, and how to stop them at any time.

Full terms are on our SMS Terms page; how we handle your data is in our Privacy Policy.

How You Opt In

Your phone number enters Ripls in one of two consented ways.

1. You give us your number

When you sign up for or log in to Ripls with your mobile number, the phone-entry screen displays this disclosure, and submitting your number is your opt-in:

By clicking Send Code, you agree to receive event invites and community updates from Ripls. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

The opening phrase names what you are signing up for at that moment, so the disclosure always matches what brought you to Ripls: event invites and community updates from an event invite, help requests and community updates from a neighbor's request for help, item updates and community updates from a shared item, or community updates when you join a community directly. Everything after that phrase — message frequency, rates, and the STOP / HELP instructions — is identical on every screen.

The screenshots above are provided so the opt-in disclosure and flow can be reviewed directly; on the live screen, submitting the phone number is the opt-in.

2. Someone you know invites you

A Ripls host can invite people they personally know by entering a phone number. Before any message is sent, the host completes a logged, timestamped attestation:

"I know these people and they're OK receiving an invite from me on Ripls."

The person then receives a single invitation that clearly identifies the host and Ripls and includes opt-out instructions. They are never added to a marketing list and are not contacted again if they opt out.

What Messages You'll Receive

Ripls only sends transactional, relationship-based messages — never marketing. Examples:

Event invite: "Alex invited you to "Backyard BBQ" on Ripls — Sat Jun 21, 4pm. View & RSVP: https://ripls.app/go/AB12CD34 Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
RSVP / offer update: "Ripls: Sam RSVP'd yes to "Backyard BBQ". See who's coming: https://ripls.app/go/AB12CD34 Reply STOP to opt out."
Community activity: "New on Ripls for "Backyard BBQ": Jordan offered to bring chairs. https://ripls.app/go/XR42KQ7 Reply STOP to opt out."
Request / giveaway: "Maria needs a ladder on Ripls and thought you might help. View: https://ripls.app/go/9KD3MQ2 Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

Message frequency varies based on your account and community activity. Message and data rates may apply.

How to Opt Out or Get Help

  • Stop all messages: reply STOP (also STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT).
  • Get help: reply HELP (or INFO), or email support@ripls.org.
  • Resume messages: reply START (or YES, UNSTOP).

Replying STOP unsubscribes you promptly — and in any event within 10 business days — and we will not text that number again, even at another member's request.

Your Privacy

Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, excluding the aggregators and carriers that deliver the messages. Your phone number is used solely to operate the messaging features described here. See our Privacy Policy for details.
