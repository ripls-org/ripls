Request: A Classroom Supply Drive

A request to stock a classroom

Maya, the teacher

Room 7 needs donations before the new school year. Ms. Okafor names the supply list in a sentence — picture books, a whiteboard, storage bins, art supplies, construction paper — and Ripls turns each item named into something a parent can claim, so five names in one line become five separate pieces. She shares it to the class parents and copies the link for the parents' WhatsApp group.

Parents offer help

Maya, the teacher

The offers come in: Theo gives a box of picture books, Nadia gives storage bins, Dana gives an art caddy, and Bea lends a whiteboard for the year. Each is tagged as given or lent on the request, so Ms. Okafor can tell what she's keeping from what goes back.

The last piece

Rob, a parent from the group chat

Rob saw the link in the parents' WhatsApp. He hasn't used Ripls before — he opens it in his browser, confirms his phone number, and takes the last open piece, a pack of construction paper.

Everything delivered

Maya, the teacher

Drop-off day: the parents bring it all by, and Ms. Okafor marks the drive done. The gifts become the classroom's to keep and the whiteboard is on loan for the year. The request keeps the record — every piece, who brought it, and an estimate of what it was worth.
