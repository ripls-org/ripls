Event: The Regular Wednesday Morning Run

Posting the run

Nadia, the host

Nadia types the run the way she'd text it: what, where, when. Ripls reads the Wednesday and the 6:30am out of the sentence and fills in the gate she meets at, and she adds a photo. Saving gives her a link to paste wherever her runners already are — a group chat, a neighborhood thread, a noticeboard.

Joining from the link

Marcus, who was sent the link

Marcus opens the link on his phone. There's no app to install and no account to make: the run's page loads in his browser, he confirms his number, gives a first name, and he's on the list. Nadia didn't need his email or have to add him to anything.

Who's coming

Nadia, the host

By Tuesday evening six people have joined, all from the same link being forwarded around. Nadia can see who's in without asking anyone to reply.

Wednesday, 6:30am

Priya, one of the runners

The run happens, and the photos go to the run's own thread rather than a group chat where they'd scroll away by lunchtime. Priya opens it afterwards and adds a note; everyone who was there is already in the conversation.

Wrapping up

Nadia, the host

Wrapping up is a confirmation rather than a form — everyone who said yes is ticked already, and Nadia unticks anyone who didn't make it. Ripls records the time the group spent together, and who came carries forward to next week.

Naming the group

Nadia, the host

The seven of them have been a group all week without a name. Nadia gives them one — Hump Day Milers — and from the group's page she copies an invite link for the group itself rather than for a single run, so whoever takes it is there for the following weeks too.

Setting up next week

Nadia, the host

Back on the home screen, Ripls offers to schedule the run again. Tapping it opens next Wednesday already filled in: the same route, the same 6:30am, and the people who actually showed up. Nadia checks it over and posts it.
